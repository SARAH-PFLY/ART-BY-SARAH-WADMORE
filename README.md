## About Sarah

**Sarah Wadmore**, also known as **Sarah PFLY** and **Sarah PU51FLY**, is an artist, creative consultant and creator of the **Crypto Moongirls** NFT collection, faction and Web3 collective.

An artist and consultant with **Graffiti Kings since 1997**, Sarah’s creative life began long before NFTs. Working from home while raising her family, she developed her art alongside motherhood and a longstanding involvement with the Graffiti Kings community. Her move into digital collectibles grew from that existing practice, bringing her artwork and ideas into new spaces.

In **2020**, Sarah created **Crypto Moongirls with Graffiti Kings**, alongside the development of **Crypto Moonboys**. The Moongirls became both a collection of digital artworks and a collective identity within the wider GK universe, connecting character design, storytelling and community.

Sarah also created **Queen Sarah PFLY**, the fictional figure who leads the Crypto Moongirls within the project’s expanding narrative. The character gives her artistic identity another form: an imagined counterpart whose story extends across artwork, digital collectibles and the lore of the Crypto Moonboys world. Sarah leads the real-world Moongirls collective, while the Queen belongs to its fictional universe.

Her involvement in early NFT culture included helping curate **Graffiti Queens**, working alongside **Graffiti Kings and Crypto Yuna**, and designing the exhibition’s promotional flyer. Opening in **Decentraland on 3 April 2021**, the exhibition brought together more than 200 women and female-identifying artists, including established crypto artists and contributors from beyond the NFT scene.

That same month, **SYNC Network** named **Sarah PU51FLY** among the artists contributing work to its Graffiti Kings-linked NFT exposition in Decentraland, alongside Crypto Yuna, Charlie Buster and Lemak Art.

Today, Sarah’s work connects her longstanding art practice with digital ownership, character creation and collective storytelling. Through her individual artwork and the Crypto Moongirls, she continues to build a creative identity rooted in the Graffiti Kings community.

## Queen Sarah PFLY — the fictional ruler of Block Topia

Queen Sarah PFLY is Sarah’s character within the **Crypto Moonboys** universe. In *The Hard Fork – Rise of the HODL Warriors*, she leads the **Crypto Moongirls**, the ruling order of **Block Topia**, a fortified city built amid the collapse of a blockchain-connected world.

Her origin centres on the **Self-Fork Project**, a lunar experiment intended to preserve human consciousness across blockchain nodes. She returns as a distributed intelligence with a human past: a **Data Sovereign** whose reach extends through the systems sustaining the city.

The Queen promises security, but her regime demands obedience. The **Hard Fork programme** and its Games turn survival into a means of selection and control. Outside her walls, the **GraffPUNKS** defend cultural memory and independence; **Jodie ZOOM 2000** emerges as a threat to the Queen’s authority.

The arrival of **NULL THE PROPHET** and the **Echo Fork** threatens the reality her systems depend upon. Her story explores identity, power and the tension between protection and freedom.

**This is fictional project lore, separate from Sarah Wadmore’s real-world biography.**

## Website

A responsive artwork gallery with three columns on desktop and two on mobile, natural artwork proportions, a black background, fullscreen viewing, video playback and individual artwork sharing.

The private Telegram bot supports artwork uploads, title-based removal and TXT/Markdown blog publishing. See [telegram/SETUP.md](telegram/SETUP.md) for commands and deployment details. Keep all credentials in encrypted Cloudflare secrets.

## Publishing

GitHub Pages publishes the website from **main → / (root)**. The canonical domain is **sarahwadmore.com**. The website is static and needs no build step. The Telegram Worker must be deployed separately with both `telegram/worker.mjs` and `telegram/blog.mjs`; preserve secrets and disabled automatic tracing.
